FFA Station Manual

How to run a lot on the FFA station, from the first fish on the scale to the PDF report. Each sample is weighed, photographed and measured by the camera, and you mark its defects with one key press.

Screens: 1920 × 1080 touch displayScale division: 1 gKeyboard + barcode reader
Step 1LotCreate it or find it, press the green arrow Step 2SamplesFish on the plate, defect keys, beep, Space Step 3BRTHang, tare, pull until the belly breaks Step 4AnisakisCount infected fish and parasites Step 5Guts weightWeigh the viscera, Finish Lot Step 6ReportExcel or PDF, then back to the lot list

01Start of shift

The station starts by itself. After power-on it waits for the FFA software and opens the screen full-size; you never need a desktop, a browser or a password. Before the first fish, check four things.

  1. Device status says ConnectedBottom-left of every screen. If it says Disconnected, wait 30 seconds; if it stays red, see Troubleshooting.
  2. The empty scale reads 0 g (±2 g)Open any lot's sample screen and look at the red/green number next to LIVE. Nothing may touch the plate.
  3. The laser cross is on the plateA green cross is shown on the live camera image. Press M to switch the laser on or off.
  4. The camera image is cleanNo water drops, fish scales or gloves in view. Wipe the plate and the lens cover if needed.
Don't put anything heavier than 5 kg on the plate, and don't lean on it. The load cell under it is a precision part.
If the empty plate reads a few grams off, take everything off, wait until the number is green, and press Z (zero) on the sample screen. If it is more than 20 g off, call a supervisor: the scale may need calibration.

02Create or pick a lot

Every sample belongs to a lot. The first screen, Lots Information, lists all lots, newest first. Use the green arrow to analyse a lot and the blue list icon to open its log.

  • 1Add creates a new lot.
  • 2Search lots filters by WMS code, lot number or supplier. The barcode reader types into this box.
  • 3Green arrow opens the lot for analysis (sample capture).
  • 4Blue list opens the lot's log: samples, extra photos and BRT results.

03Capture samples

This is where most of the shift is spent. The left side shows the live camera and live weight; the right side shows the last sample you captured.

  • 1Progress: Samples → BRT → Anisakis → Guts Weight.
  • 2Lot being analysed and the number of the last saved sample.
  • 3Status: Ready to capture, Processing…, Ready for next sample.
  • 4Live weight. Red while settling, green when stable.
  • 5Live camera with the laser cross. Lay the fish along the horizontal line.
  • 6Defect lights for the fish on the plate. Each one shows its key underneath.
  • 7Captured: the last sample's photo, weight and defects.
  • 8Log and Other Findings (extra photo, key E).
  • 9Help: shows every keyboard shortcut.
  • 10Cancel Lot leaves the lot and returns to the lot list.
  • 11Next: BRT saves the last sample and moves on.

One fish, step by step

  1. Place the fish on the plateLay it flat along the horizontal laser line, head to the left, fully inside the camera view. Take your hand off the fish.
  2. Mark its defectsPress the key of each defect you see. Its light turns from red to green. Press the same key again to undo. See the key map.
  3. Wait for the beepThe station beeps once when the weight is confirmed: three stable readings within 1 g, and at least 20 g on the plate. The live weight turns green.
  4. Press SpaceThe photo and the confirmed weight move to the Captured panel, together with the defects you marked. The camera measures length and height.
  5. Take the fish off and place the next oneThe captured sample is saved when you capture the next fish or press Next: BRT. Until then you can still discard it with C.
You may press Space before the beep. The station then waits up to 4 seconds for the weight to settle and captures the confirmed value, never a reading that was still moving. If it doesn't settle, you'll see “Weight never stabilized, capture aborted”: steady the fish and press Space again.

Other findings (extra photos)

Use it for anything worth a photo that has no defect key: a foreign object, a label problem, unusual colour. The photo goes to the lot, not to a sample, and appears under Extra Images in the log.

When you leave the sample screen with a captured sample that isn't saved yet, the station asks “Would you like to save the last test?”. Press Y to save it or N to discard it.

Don't press T (tare) or Z (zero) while a fish or a tray is on the plate. The next weights would all be wrong.

05Belly resistance test

The BRT measures how much pull the belly wall takes before it tears. It uses the hook load cell, not the plate. The result is linked to the last sample you captured.

  • 1State: Waiting, Arming, Running, Finished.
  • 2Test duration in seconds.
  • 3Max tension: the break point in grams.
  • 4Live curve of the pull.
  • 5Start/Stop test (Space).
  • 6Reset (C) and Save (S).
  1. Hang the fish on the hookLet it hang still.
  2. Press T to tareHere the tare is correct: it removes the fish's own weight, so only the pull is measured.
  3. Pull steadilyThe test starts by itself once the pull rises 30 g above the start. You can also press Space to start it.
  4. Keep pulling until the belly tearsThe test ends when the force drops to half of its peak for half a second (peak at least 80 g), or after 10 seconds.
  5. Save with S“Added” confirms it. C resets to try again. Pressing Space after a finished test saves it and starts the next one.
Next: Anisakis saves a finished, unsaved test before moving on. H or Back: Samples returns to sample capture. While a test runs, every key except Space is ignored.

06Anisakis inspection

Count the fish you opened and, for each body site, how many fish had parasites and how many parasites you found. The results update while you count.

  • 1Fish analyzed: how many fish this inspection covers. Usually 10.
  • 2Sites: Guts (viscera), Belly (flaps and cavity wall), Embedded (inside the muscle). Each has Fish with anisakis and Anisakis found.
  • 3Warnings flag counts that can't be right, e.g. an infected fish with 0 parasites.
  • 4Results: prevalence and intensity per site.
  • 5Save inspection. The line below says Not saved yet or Saved.
FigureFormulaExample (10 fish, 2 infected, 5 parasites)
Prevalenceinfected fish ÷ fish analyzed2 ÷ 10 = 20.0 %
Intensityparasites found ÷ infected fish5 ÷ 2 = 2.50 per infected fish
A lot with no parasites is a valid result: save it with all counts at 0. The big button reads Save & Continue while there are unsaved counts and Next: Guts Weight once they are saved.

07Guts weight

Weigh the viscera of the analysed fish together on the plate.

  1. Put the guts on the plate (in a tared tray if you use one)Wait for Stable.
  2. Press Capture current weightThe value goes into the field. You can correct it by hand.
  3. Press Save Guts Weight“Saved: … g” appears below.
  4. Press Finish LotThe lot is complete.

08Finish & reports

  • Download Excel: every sample of the lot as an .xlsx sheet.
  • Download FFA Report: the full quality-control report as a PDF.
  • View Logs: samples, extra photos and BRT results.
  • Back to Home: the lot list, ready for the next lot.
Reports can be downloaded again at any time from Configuration → Export data.

09Lot log & samples

Open a log from the blue list icon in the lot list, from Log on the sample screen, or from View Logs when a lot is complete.

10Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
No beep, and Space says “Weight never stabilized”Something is touching the fish or the plate, or the fish is under 20 g. Let go, check nothing leans on the plate, press Space again.
The empty plate doesn't read 0 gClear the plate, wait for green, press Z. More than 20 g off: call a supervisor.
Red chip next to the weight (e.g. ADC_FAILURE) or orange NO LINKThe scale transmitter reports a fault or doesn't answer. Captures are blocked. Call a supervisor.
“Scale fault: …” after SpaceSame as above: the capture was refused on purpose. Nothing was saved.
Device Status: DisconnectedWait 30 s. If it stays red, restart the station (power off, wait 10 s, power on). The screen comes back by itself.
Black camera imageCheck the camera cable and the lighting. If the station was just restarted, wait a minute.
A key marks the wrong defectPress it again to clear it. Defects are only saved with the next capture.
The window closed or shows a desktopThe screen reopens by itself within a few seconds. If it doesn't, restart the station.
Captured the wrong fishPress C before you capture the next fish or leave the screen. Once saved, ask a supervisor.

13Scale calibration Supervisor

Calibrate the plate when the empty reading drifts by more than 20 g, after moving the station, after any repair, and when a known weight reads wrong. You need the 1000 g reference weight and an empty plate.

Don't cancel halfway unless you must. Cancelling ends the session but does not undo what the transmitter already applied; run the full calibration again afterwards.
If the wizard stops with “The transmitter is showing NET weight”, a tare was set from the transmitter's own keypad. Press Disable tare in the dialog, then start again.

After calibrating

  1. Check zeroEmpty plate: 0 g, green.
  2. Check the referencePut the 1000 g weight in the centre: 1000 g ±1 g.
  3. Check the cornersThe same weight on each corner of the plate should read within 2 g.

14Camera zone & length Supervisor

Recalibrate length whenever the camera or the plate is moved. Check it by measuring an object of known length as a sample.

17Kiosk maintenance Technician

The station screen is locked to the FFA application: Alt+F4, the Windows key and Ctrl+Alt+T do nothing, and if the window is closed it reopens. To work on the desktop, connect by SSH and run:

CommandEffect
~/.local/bin/ffa-kiosk-stopCloses the kiosk until the next restart.
sudo rebootRestarts; the kiosk comes back by itself.
~/Documents/FFA-complete-project/ffa-app/tools/kiosk/install.sh --uninstallRemoves the kiosk and restores the normal desktop.
